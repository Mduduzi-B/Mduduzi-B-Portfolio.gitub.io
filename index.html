01 — Online Retail Sales Analysis

Business & Customer Analytics

Analysed over 536,000 transaction records for a UK-based online retailer to understand revenue trends, customer behaviour, product performance and returns. The analysis combined data cleaning, exploratory analysis and business recommendations to identify opportunities for customer retention, inventory planning and international growth.

Tools: Python · Excel · Data Cleaning · Exploratory Data Analysis · Data Visualisation

Key insight: The analysis revealed strong seasonal demand, significant customer concentration, a substantial revenue impact from returns, and a large portion of revenue that could not be attributed to a known customer.

02 — Food Delivery Operations Analysis

Operations & Data Analytics

Analysed 10,000 South African food-delivery orders to identify the factors affecting delivery performance and investigate a cancellation rate of approximately 33.5%. The project involved data cleaning, exploratory analysis, Python, SQL and business-focused recommendations.

Tools: Python · Pandas · SQL · Excel · Data Cleaning · Exploratory Data Analysis

Key insight: Cancellations were the biggest operational issue, while delivery time showed almost no relationship with distance or restaurant preparation time. This shifted the focus from assumed routing and kitchen problems toward cancellation processes and gaps in operational data.
