01

Online Retail Sales Analysis

BUSINESS & CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

Analysed more than 536,000 transaction records for a UK-based online retailer to understand revenue trends, customer behaviour, product performance and returns.

The analysis combined data cleaning, exploratory analysis and business-focused recommendations to identify opportunities for customer retention, inventory planning and international growth.

Key Insight

The analysis revealed strong seasonal demand, significant customer concentration, a substantial revenue impact from returns, and a large portion of revenue that could not be attributed to a known customer.

Tools: Python · Pandas · Excel · Data Cleaning · EDA · Data Visualisation

02

Food Delivery Operations Analysis

OPERATIONS & DATA ANALYTICS

Analysed 10,000 South African food-delivery orders to identify factors affecting delivery performance and investigate a cancellation rate of approximately 33.5%.

The project involved data cleaning, exploratory analysis, Python, SQL and business-focused recommendations.

Key Insight

Cancellations were the biggest operational issue, while delivery time showed almost no relationship with distance or restaurant preparation time.

Tools: Python · Pandas · SQL · Excel · Data Cleaning · EDA
